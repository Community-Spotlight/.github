# FAQ
## How can I submit My Promotions?
Please fill out [this form](https://community-spotlight.github.io/uploader-site/), a **Team Member** will eventually look over and possibly add your Promotion.

Guidelines are listed in the above site. Simply click on the "See Guidelines" text in the *Promotional Media* section.

## How can I display other peoples Promotions?
View our [Developer Exports](https://community-spotlight.github.io/?page=exports). There is documentation on how to set up and use it.

Please display as much as you can, it truely helps other small creators. :)

## My Promotion stopped showing up, what happened?
Once your Promotion is added to our system, it will be **deleted in 2 weeks.** This is to help manage storage space...

You are more than welcome to re-submit the [form](https://community-spotlight.github.io/uploader-site/) to add your Promotion back.
